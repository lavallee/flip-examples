# NJ enrollment, cast forward

The companion to [nj-schools](../nj-schools/): that notebook proved what
statewide enrollment *did* (dip −0.98%, recovery by 2023-24, then a
−1.63% slide); this one commits to what the same watched surface will
show next. A backward notebook fights staleness; this one accrues
credibility through resolution — every forecast is dated, annullable,
and resolves on what the desk can actually see at the DOE index.

The naive baseline is declared in [baseline.md](baseline.md) *before*
anything resolves — the only time declaring it is worth anything. FC2 and
FC3 are deliberately near-dated calibration questions (the record needs
volume before Brier means anything); FC1 is the headline. FC3 doubles as
a live test of the silent-revision behavior nj-schools documented: it
resolves by re-fetching the 2025-26 ZIP and comparing bytes against the
hash we captured on 2026-07-25.

`bears_on` refs point across the workspace at nj-schools' claims and open
question — the local doctor counts them as dangling (legal, like dangling
citations); `flip resolve nj-schools:C3` finds them from the workspace
root.
