# Criteria — frozen before screening began

Question: **does retrieval augmentation measurably reduce hallucination in
LLM outputs — and how do the studies that say so operationalize
"hallucination"?**

Frozen 2026-07-26, before the first search was run. Amendments after this
point are criteria-amendment edition triggers, never silent edits.

## Inclusion

- **I1 — empirical comparison.** The paper reports a quantitative
  hallucination/factuality measurement for retrieval-augmented generation
  against a non-retrieval baseline (same or comparable model family).
- **I2 — operationalization stated.** The paper defines how hallucination
  was measured (metric, judge, benchmark) concretely enough to extract.
- **I3 — period.** First posted 2020-01-01 through 2026-07-25.
- **I4 — rights.** License permits redistribution of the captured PDF in a
  public custody notebook (CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0). A custody constraint of
  THIS notebook, not a quality judgment — I4 exclusions are recorded as
  rights-excluded, distinct from editorial exclusion.

## Exclusion

- **E1 — no baseline.** Retrieval evaluated with no non-retrieval
  comparison.
- **E2 — surveys/position papers.** Kept as context sources (graded, never
  extracted, never corroborating the effect claims).
- **E3 — mitigation-stacked.** Retrieval inseparable from other
  interventions, making the retrieval effect unattributable.
- **E4 — non-redistributable license** (see I4).
