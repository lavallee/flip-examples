Did NJ public school enrollment dip after 2019-20, and has it recovered by 2025-26?

## Answer
Yes and no: enrollment dipped 0.98% in 2020-21 (C1), recovered to 0.30% above the pre-pandemic level by 2023-24 (C2), then fell again — 2025-26 sits 1.34% below 2019-20, a decline now larger than the pandemic dip (C3).
