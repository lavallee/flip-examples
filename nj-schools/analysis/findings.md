# Findings — the dip, the recovery, and the decline after

*The notebook's own write-up: the completed work, living beside the
sources and claims it depends on. The public render of this page is
[njschooldata.fyi/reports/statewide-enrollment/](https://njschooldata.fyi/reports/statewide-enrollment/);
this file is canonical — corrections land here first.*

"NJ school enrollment dipped in the pandemic" is repeated as settled
background in budget and facilities arguments. The state's own fall
headcounts say something more interesting.

**The pandemic dip was real but modest.** Statewide fall enrollment fell
13,428.5 students (−0.98%) from 2019-20 to 2020-21 [C1]. A one-percent
single-year drop was unusual, but it was not a collapse.

**It came back — completely.** By fall 2023-24 the statewide count stood
at 1,379,988.0 — 0.30% *above* the pre-pandemic 2019-20 level [C2].
"Enrollment never recovered from COVID" is, statewide, false.

**The decline that followed is the story.** Fall 2025-26 enrollment is
1,357,449.5 — down 1.63% from 2023-24 and 1.34% below the pre-pandemic
baseline [C3]. In two years New Jersey lost more enrollment than it did
in the pandemic year. What drives it is the open question [Q2], and the
companion forward notebook
([nj-enrollment-forward](../../nj-enrollment-forward/)) has committed to
what next year's file will show.

| Fall of school year | Statewide enrollment | vs previous captured year | vs 2019-20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-20 | 1,375,828.5 | — | — |
| 2020-21 | 1,362,400.0 | −13,428.5 (−0.98%) | −0.98% |
| 2023-24 | 1,379,988.0 | +17,588.0 (+1.29%) | +0.30% |
| 2025-26 | 1,357,449.5 | −22,538.5 (−1.63%) | −1.34% |

Every number is the State sheet's "All Grades" row from a hashed capture
of the NJ DOE file ([A1]–[A4]), cross-checked by independently summing
the District sheet — exact agreement, to the half-student, in all four
years. Half-students are real: the DOE counts shared-time vocational
students as 0.5. Four captured years, not a continuous series — 2021-22,
2022-23, and 2024-25 would refine the shape of the turn.
